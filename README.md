# 👋 Hi, I’m Matti Seise (@mattiseise)

I’m a vocational special education teacher, computer science educator, and GenAI trainer/consultant based in Helsinki, Finland.

I have an MSc in Computer Science and have worked in education since 2011. My work sits at the intersection of software, AI, accessibility, learning support, and practical automation.

A lot of what I build starts from the same question: **can technology remove unnecessary friction without removing the thinking that actually matters?**

## 🧠 What I do

- Teach upper secondary vocational ICT: programming, web technologies, Python, JavaScript, cybersecurity, IT support, and software development
- Design accessible and scaffolded learning environments for students with diverse learning needs
- Train teachers and other professionals in practical and critical use of generative AI
- Build AI agents, multi-agent workflows, and AI-assisted production pipelines
- Automate repetitive work with Python, browser automation, and small purpose-built tools
- Develop open educational materials and practical AI literacy resources

## 🤖 Currently focused on

- Agentic AI systems and persistent AI agents for real-world workflows
- Multi-agent pipelines for research, content production, quality control, and educational material development
- Using AI as cognitive scaffolding rather than a replacement for thinking
- AI-supported accessibility and differentiated instruction
- Local LLMs and privacy-conscious AI use in education
- Building workflows where humans remain responsible for goals, judgment, and final decisions

## 🧭 How I think about AI

I’m interested in AI as a **cognitive offloading and scaffolding tool**.

That means using AI to handle things such as formatting, translation, restructuring, searching, repetitive operations, and first drafts — while keeping understanding, judgment, verification, and decision-making with the human.

In education, I’m particularly interested in finding the line between **supporting the learner** and **doing the learning for them**.
